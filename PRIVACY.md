# Privacy Policy

**Luma11y** — published by [WebAccessibilityTools](https://github.com/WebAccessibilityTools)
Last updated: 2026-11-04

## Summary

Luma11y does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. Everything the app does happens on your computer.

## Data processed locally

- **Colours and settings**: the colours you enter or pick, your copy templates, shortcuts, language and display preferences are saved locally on your device so they can be restored on the next launch. They never leave your computer.
- **Screen colour picker**: to pick a colour, Luma11y reads the pixels around the mouse pointer. These pixels are displayed in the magnifier and discarded immediately. No screenshot is saved or sent anywhere. On macOS, this requires the *Screen Recording* permission, which you can revoke at any time in System Settings.

## Network access

- **Microsoft Store version**: the app makes no network connection. Updates are delivered by the Microsoft Store.
- **GitHub releases version**: the app may check for updates by downloading a small file from `github.com`. This request is subject to [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). You can disable the automatic check in *Settings → Updates*.

No other network request is made. Luma11y contains no analytics, telemetry, advertising or crash reporting.

## Third parties

Luma11y does not share any data with third parties.

## Children

Luma11y does not collect data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

This policy may be updated when the app changes. The current version is always available at
<https://github.com/WebAccessibilityTools/Luma11y/blob/main/PRIVACY.md>.
